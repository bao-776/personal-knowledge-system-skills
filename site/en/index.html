Original input

Prioritize: the origin of 45% and the claim that terminology caused delay. Preserve as opinion: the author’s interface preference. Skip: background unrelated to this task.

Raw/test-log.md

Inspect evidence

Fictional test log: three pauses across five tests. No terminology control group or timing result supports 45%. Status: observation supported; cause and percentage unresolved.

Missing evidence is not disproof. Repeated copies are not independent verification.

Wiki/setup-pauses.md

Maintain a note

Three of five fictional testers paused during setup. Terminology is one possible explanation; causality and effect size remain unresolved. Sources: meeting note + test log. Preserve 45% as an attributed unchecked claim.

Maintain a bounded conclusion linked to its original evidence.

knowledge-retrieve

Retrieve it

Question: can we confirm a 45% increase due to terminology? Answer: no. Inspected records support pauses but provide no timing or control evidence for 45%. Source: Wiki/setup-pauses.md → Raw/test-log.md.

Return supporting sources and gaps together; do not invent an answer.

knowledge-impact

Trace the impact

Proposed: replace “terminology caused 45% delay” in Outputs/team-brief.md with the bounded observation. Preserve: original meeting quote and dated snapshots. State: proposal only; no files edited.

A changed conclusion does not authorize editing every dependent file.

Keep Raw unchanged, revise Wiki with evidence, and propose updates to Outputs.

Repository and full documentation

Capabilities and their limits.

Does it verify every sentence?

No. It first selects key premises. The default is three claim groups and two search rounds each; unresolved premises stay explicit and originals remain unchanged.

Does it crawl my saved social posts?

No background account scanning. You select one link, screenshot or text. Access barriers are respected. Computer use does not guarantee avoiding platform detection.

Is this an Obsidian plugin?

No. It is a Markdown-first Codex skill suite; Obsidian properties, links and Bases are optional enhancements.

What has actually been verified?

Automated checks cover structure, links, installation consistency, failure recovery and evaluation manifests. Live model behavior still needs evaluation; this fictional example is not an execution result.

Read the evaluation protocol

Where do the methods come from?

Core modules draw on provenance, atomic notes, human-centered design and reflective practice. These explain design choices, not proven effectiveness of the complete system.
