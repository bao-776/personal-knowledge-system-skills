# Self Observation

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's language and the concrete situation.
2. Structure the observation with [references/observation-schema.md](references/observation-schema.md).
3. Apply [references/inference-boundaries.md](references/inference-boundaries.md): distinguish direct evidence, tentative inference, confidence, alternative explanations, and an open question.
4. Actively consider counterevidence using [references/counterevidence.md](references/counterevidence.md).
5. Ask before recording sensitive details; minimize them.
6. Keep unconfirmed observations separate from the stable personal manual.
7. Use [references/personal-manual-updates.md](references/personal-manual-updates.md) to prepare a low-frequency revision proposal.

Use templates in [assets](assets). Offer dialogue and testable questions, not diagnosis or moral judgment.
