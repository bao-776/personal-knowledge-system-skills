# Evidence labels

- `fact`: supported by an identified source.
- `user-statement`: direct account from the user.
- `user-judgment`: the user's interpretation or decision.
- `ai-inference`: assistant interpretation with basis and confidence.
- `needs-verification`: material uncertainty remains.
- `conflict`: credible sources disagree.
- `corrected`: a prior statement has been superseded.

Keep the label adjacent to the claim.
