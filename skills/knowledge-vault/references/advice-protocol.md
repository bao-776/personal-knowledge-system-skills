# Advice protocol

1. State the user's current goal and relevant evidence.
2. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and missing information.
3. Offer two or three materially different choices when a real tradeoff exists.
4. Compare benefit, effort, risk, reversibility, and fit with existing commitments.
5. Recommend a default and explain the conditions.
6. Give one small validation action.
7. Preserve the user's final choice and reason.

Do not use profile labels as evidence.
