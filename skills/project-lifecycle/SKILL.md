# Project Lifecycle

## Workflow

1. Establish goal, completion criteria, scope, constraints, owner, and next action.
2. Track states with [references/project-states.md](references/project-states.md).
3. Separate confirmed facts, decisions, open questions, sources, results, and capability evidence.
4. Record significant choices with [references/decision-and-evidence.md](references/decision-and-evidence.md).
5. When work resumes after interruption, use [references/interruption-and-resume.md](references/interruption-and-resume.md).
6. At completion or termination, produce a factual summary and route reflection to the retrospective skill.
7. Report actual validation and remaining uncertainty.

Use the templates in [assets](assets). Preserve attribution among the user, collaborators, automation, and AI.
